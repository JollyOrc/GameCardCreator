# M06 — The creature card in Obsidian with the real Obsidian2Typst (US-004 AC-3, AC-9, AC-10, AC-11, AC-12, AC-12a, AC-13)

Part of the [manual checks](../manual-checks.md).

**When:** after ticket #26 on a test build; and after any change to the creature card, the compile
with files and fonts, or the call of Obsidian2Typst's API.

**Needs:** Obsidian desktop (the author's default environment), the scratch vault of Obsidian2Typst
(`~/Documents/Obsidian2Typst/spikes/typst-wasm/scratch-vault`) with **Obsidian2Typst 0.15.1** (its
release build, preset `Cards M29`, four-to-five generated icon shapes and the credit text `Icons:
generated shapes, M29`) and the Game Card Creator test build. Prepared by the developer (SCAR-012,
SCAR-018): the notes in `M06 Creatures`, `M06 Skills`, `M06 Spells`, `M06 Decks` (invented), the
plugin's `data.json` (rules: `M06 Creatures` creature, `M06 Skills` skill, `M06 Spells` spell; output
folder `M06 Out` inside the vault), the starting checksums in `spikes/m06-before.txt` and the touched
files in `spikes/m06-state-backup/`. The developer waits for the tester's "go" after each block of steps.
**Restart Obsidian once** so both plugins are loaded.

**Expected versions:** Game Card Creator `0.2.0-m06.2`, Obsidian2Typst `0.15.1` (Settings, Community
plugins). If either differs, stop: the wrong build is installed. In Obsidian2Typst's settings the
active preset is `Cards M29`.

**Not assumed (SCAR-024, SCAR-045):** how long the first creature compile takes inside Obsidian (it
builds a compiler with the book's fonts; measured in Node only), and that the icon files and fonts
reach the compile inside Obsidian. The expected cards and warnings below come from a rehearsal with
the real Obsidian2Typst code, this vault's preset and these notes, compiled in Node (SCAR-019); what
Obsidian shows is what this check records.

## Steps

1. Right-click the folder `M06 Creatures`, choose `Create cards`. Note roughly how long it takes
   (seconds, from the click to the notice). **Expected notice:** `7 cards on 1 page, 7 warnings.
   Written to …/M06 Out`. Files: `M06 Out/M06 Creatures cards.pdf` and `M06 Creatures cards warnings.md`.
2. Open the PDF. **Expected, card by card (one page, cards in the order of the file names):**
   1. *Bog Titan:* a statblock (30 boxes in two rows, Might 14 · Wits 6 · Speed 10, Slam 3d6, Crush 2d8,
      Magical), **no Skills line, no Behaviour, no Signs**, only the Actions text, small (5.5 pt), cut
      off at the bottom of the card.
   2. *Fen Hare:* the statblock with 11 boxes, four state cells (circle, square, triangle, bar), Might
      4 · Wits 2 · Speed 5, Kick 2d6, **Bite** (bold) 1d6, `Skills: Quick Step`, Beast; then
      **Behaviour** (two bullets), **Actions** (two bullets), **Signs** (one line).
   3. *Marsh Boar:* the statblock with 20 boxes, Move 8 squares · Guard 4, three attacks, `Skills:
      Charge, Thick Hide`; the Actions text; **no Behaviour, no Signs**.
   4. *Miscounted Newt:* a statblock with 9 boxes, Behaviour and Actions.
   5. *Mist Wisp:* **no statblock**: the name as a bold title with a line, then Behaviour and Actions.
   6. *Quiet Moth:* the statblock, then Actions only (no Behaviour section in the note).
   7. *Reed Heron:* the statblock with **three** state cells (circle, square, bar), Behaviour, Actions.
   At the foot of the page, centred in the margin, one small line: `Icons: generated shapes, M29`.
   Report anything that differs.
3. Look at **printed size** (zoom 100 %, or print preview) and at **400 %** on the statblock of Fen Hare
   (AC-10, SCAR-025): the text of the statblock and of the sections is readable, nothing is clipped
   except the end of Bog Titan's Actions, the headings are bold and clearly apart from their text.
4. Open `M06 Creatures cards warnings.md`. **Expected, seven lines** (the order may differ):
   `STAT_SUM_MISMATCH` (Miscounted Newt: `9 ≠ 2+2+2`), `CREATURE_STATBLOCK_REFUSED` (Mist Wisp: `No
   statblock: no stats or attacks.`), `CREATURE_SECTION_MISSING` (Quiet Moth: `Behaviour`),
   `CREATURE_PART_DROPPED` (Bog Titan: `Instincts`; Bog Titan: `Skills`; Marsh Boar: `Instincts`),
   `CREATURE_TEXT_OVERFLOW` (Bog Titan: `5.5 pt`).
5. Right-click `M06 Decks/Mixed Deck.md`, choose `Create cards from this deck note`. **Expected:** `4
   cards on 1 page, 0 warnings`; a skill card (Reed Sweep), Fen Hare, a spell card (Reed Whisper), Reed
   Heron, in that order; the credit line is at the foot of the page.
6. Run step 1 again (answer `Replace`). **Expected:** the same notice and the same cards; it is not
   noticeably slower than the first time (report both times). The icon cells still show their shapes
   and the fonts are the same (nothing of the first compile is missing in the second).
7. Settings, Community plugins: switch **Obsidian2Typst off**. Right-click `M06 Creatures`, `Create
   cards`. **Expected notice:** `There are no cards to print: Creature cards need Obsidian2Typst 0.15.1
   or newer, installed and enabled`, and no file is written. Right-click `M06 Decks/Mixed Deck.md`, `Create
   cards from this deck note`: **expected** a PDF with the skill and spell card only (2 cards) and a
   warnings file with one line, `CREATURE_API_UNAVAILABLE` (empty path). Switch Obsidian2Typst on again.
8. Settings, Game Card Creator, page `Note properties`: under `Creature notes` three fields (`Instincts
   heading`, `Actions heading`, `Signs heading`) with `Behaviour`, `Actions`, `Signs`. Set `Actions
   heading` to `Moves`. Right-click the note `M06 Creatures/Fen Hare.md`, `Create cards`. **Expected:**
   one card, **one warning** `CREATURE_SECTION_MISSING` (detail `Moves`), the card without an Actions
   section. Then set the field back to `Actions` and run again: the Actions section is back, no warning.
   Try an empty value: **expected** a red message and nothing saved.
9. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report it even if it seems
   unrelated (SCAR-015).

**The developer's undo (SCAR-018):** the vault is compared with `spikes/m06-before.txt`; the files
this check added (the folders `M06 *`, `.obsidian/plugins/game-card-creator/`, the plugin's line in
`community-plugins.json`, the Obsidian2Typst manifest changed from `0.15.1-m29.1` to `0.15.1`, the
