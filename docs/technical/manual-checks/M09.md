`M09 Spells` spell; output folder `M09 Out` inside the vault; scheme note `M09 Scheme.md`; every other

## Steps

**Block A — today's look (both switches off)**

1. Settings, Community plugins: **Game Card Creator `0.5.0-m09.1`**.
2. Right-click the folder `M09 Skills`, choose `Create cards`. **Expected notice:** `6 cards on 1 page, 1
   warning. Written to …/M09 Out`. Files: `M09 Out/M09 Skills cards.pdf` (rehearsal **25 254 bytes**) and
   `M09 Skills cards warnings.md` with the one line `SKILL_BRANCH_UNKNOWN — M09 Skills/Plain.md — Misc`.
3. Open the PDF. **Expected (today):** six cards; five with a band and a sword, *Plain* with a plain header
   and a rule. The bands are **not** equally high: *Riverbank Ambush of the Reed Wardens* and *The Long
   Riverbank Ambush …* wrap their titles, *Feint* its tags line. The title of *Long Lesson* (more text,
   6 pt) is smaller than the others'.

**Block B — the switch on an open tab (AC-10, SCAR-016)**

4. Settings, Game Card Creator, `Page and text size`. **Expected:** after `Text size` two groups, `Skill
   titles` and `Spell titles`, each with only the toggle `Equal title bars` (off). Keep this tab open until
   step 9.
5. Switch **`Skill titles` → `Equal title bars` on**. **Expected at once** (without leaving the page):
   under it `Title size` **9.1** and `Smallest title size` **7**; the spell group still shows only its toggle.
6. In `Title size` type `21` and press Enter. **Expected:** refused, the message under the field `The skill
   title size 21 is outside 6 to 20 pt`. Type `9.1` again and press Enter: the message goes.

**Block C — equal title bars (AC-2 to AC-7)**

7. Run `M09 Skills` again (answer `Replace`). Note roughly how long it takes. **Expected notice:** `6 cards
   on 1 page, 2 warnings.` Warnings file, in this order:
   - `SKILL_BRANCH_UNKNOWN — M09 Skills/Plain.md — Misc`
   - `SKILL_TITLE_WRAPPED — M09 Skills/The Long Riverbank Ambush of the Hidden Reed Wardens.md — 1 of 6
     titles wrap at 7 pt`

   PDF: rehearsal **25 040 bytes**.
8. Open the PDF. **Expected:** every title the **same size** (7 pt, smaller than in step 3); the long title
   still on two lines; the **five bands equally high** (the height of the long title's header); *Plain*'s
   rule at the height where the band of a banded card would end its header; *Long Lesson*'s title as large
   as the others (its text is smaller, about 5.75 pt). Look at the top row **at printed size and at 400 %**
   on the lower edges of neighbouring bands (SCAR-025): one line.

**Block D — no room to shrink, a hidden value kept (AC-5, AC-9, AC-10)**

9. On the open tab: `Smallest title size` `9.1`, Enter. Run `M09 Skills` (`Replace`). **Expected:** `6
   cards on 1 page, 2 warnings`; the second line now `… — 2 of 6 titles wrap at 9.1 pt` (the widest title's
   note as before); PDF rehearsal **25 053 bytes**; in the PDF the titles are larger (9.1 pt), *Riverbank
   Ambush* wraps beside its sword, all bands taller and still equal.
10. Switch `Equal title bars` **off**: the two size fields vanish at once. Switch it **on** again: `Smallest
    title size` shows **9.1** (kept). Set it back to `7`, Enter.

**Block E — spells, and off again (AC-1, D1)**

11. Switch **`Spell titles` → `Equal title bars` on**. Run `M09 Spells`. **Expected:** `3 cards on 1 page,
    0 warnings`, PDF rehearsal **21 002 bytes**; the three titles the same size (7.35 pt: the size at which
    *Slow Ember Curse of the Ashen Marsh* fits beside its fire icon); the three bands equally high.
12. Switch **both** toggles off. Run `M09 Skills` (`Replace`). **Expected:** the notice and the warnings
    file of step 2, and the PDF **25 254 bytes** again (today's cards exactly, AC-1).
13. Is anything missing, odd or ugly that this list did not ask about? Report it even if it seems unrelated
    (SCAR-015).

## Results

Build `0.5.0-m09.1`, the author's run of 2026-11-16, default environment: "everything works as expected",
with **one finding** (block E, with a picture of `Glow`): on the spell cards, whose titles share 7.35 pt, the
band ends **above the bottom of the fire icon**; the author asks for padding under the icon in such cases,
**also with equal title bars off**.

- *Measured afterwards (developer, Typst CLI 0.14.2, the plugin's icon font at 18 pt):* the band is 4 mm + the
  header + 2 mm (US-009 AC-3) and the icon starts at the header's top, so an icon taller than the header + 2 mm
  sticks out. `Glow`'s header is 3.41 mm (tags and title at 7.35 pt), the band ends 9.41 mm from the card's top,
  the fire icon (5.84 mm) at 9.84 mm. The 494 icons of the font are 4.2 to 6.4 mm high. With the switches off a
  spell header at 7 pt is 4.05 mm (the fire just fits); a spell whose text shrinks to 6 pt has a header of about
  3.5 mm, so the defect is not new in 0.5.0: it is in US-009's rule (its G4 had recorded "the icon never makes
  the band taller" as a finding).
- Not recorded: the step times (the author reported no problem).
- *Disk, read before the re-check (developer):* the last skills PDF (16:39, 25 254 bytes) is the switches-off
  result of steps 2 and 12; steps 7 and 9 would have written 25 040 and 25 053 bytes, and no skills PDF was written
  after 16:39. The spells PDF (17:18, 21 002 bytes) equals step 11's rehearsal. The settings file still had both
  switches on and `Smallest title size` 9.1 for skills, so steps 10 and 12 were not done as written. Whether steps
  7 and 9 ran is not known; the re-check runs blocks B to E again.
