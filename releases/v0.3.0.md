# Game Card Creator v0.3.0

**Release date:** 2026-11-16

Colour-coded headers and icons on skill and spell cards, read from a scheme note of your vault. Nothing
changes for a vault that does not set the new **Scheme note** setting: the cards are made exactly as in
0.2.0. Desktop only.

## Features

- **Scheme note** (US-009): a setting on the page `Note properties` names an ordinary note of your vault
  (a vault path). Its first table with the columns `Tag`, `Colour` (`#rrggbb`) and `Icon` (a name from
  the RPG Awesome icon font) is the scheme; the front matter may hold `schemeVersion: 1`, `iconSet:
  rpg-awesome` and `credit`. The note is read at every run and never changed.
- **Colour band and icons:** a skill or spell card whose tags have rows in the scheme gets its header (tags,
  title, details row) on a band from the top edge to both sides, in the colour of its branch, with the
  branch's icon (18 pt, black) at the right. Several branches make a gradient from the first colour to the
  last and several icons, in the order of the note's tags. The title and the tags line wrap before the
  icons and the band grows with them. The text starts 2 mm under the band and keeps its size rules; on the
  author's 174 skill and spell notes no card shrinks more than before. Creature cards are unchanged.
- **Bleed:** the setting `Bleed` (page `Layout`, 0 to 3 mm, default 0; at most 1 mm while cut marks are on)
  lets the band reach beyond the card's frame on the sides where no card is next to it, and half the gap
  where one is.
- **Credit:** a line at the foot of each sheet that shows an icon (5 pt, centred, at most two lines); with a
  creature credit on the same sheet the two are one text, the creature credit first. The default text
  names the icon authors as Game-icons.net asks (`Icons made by Lorc, Delapouite, sbed and other authors.
  Available on https://game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0), via RPG Awesome`); the scheme note's `credit` replaces
  it, an empty `credit` prints no line.
- **Warnings:** a note with no branch in the scheme gets `SKILL_BRANCH_UNKNOWN` or `SPELL_BRANCH_UNKNOWN`
  (detail: its tags); a problem with the scheme note is `SCHEME_NOTE_UNREADABLE`, `SCHEME_UNSUPPORTED`,
  `SCHEME_TABLE_MISSING` or `SCHEME_ROW_INVALID` (with the line of the row). None of them stops a run.
- **The icon font** RPG Awesome (SIL OFL 1.1; glyph designs from Game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0) is inside
  `main.js` with the other font; its licence texts are in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Changed behaviour

- A card with no branch in the scheme, and every card when the setting is empty, is made as before.
- The settings screen has a field `Scheme note` (with a line saying whether the note is found) and a field
  `Bleed`.

## Fixes

None.

## Breaking changes and migration

None. To use it: write a scheme note (a table of tag, colour, icon), set `Scheme note` to its vault path.

## Known limits

- **Tested on:** Obsidian 1.15.0 on macOS (Apple silicon): manual check M07 in a scratch vault with
  Obsidian2Typst 0.15.1, and the author's own vault. **Not tested:** Windows, Linux, mobile, other Obsidian
  versions. `minAppVersion` stays 1.6.6.
- The default credit text names Lorc, Delapouite and sbed; other Game-icons.net authors may have drawn
  icons you use: set your own `credit` in the scheme note.
- The band is exactly as high as the header plus 2 mm; a header lower than the icons lets the icon reach the
  band's bottom edge. A banded card has no rule under the header.
- No picture on the creature card (US-006). Backlog #5: no warning yet when a card's text has a character
  the font lacks.
- `main.js` is about 15.8 MB (the compiler and two fonts are inside it).
