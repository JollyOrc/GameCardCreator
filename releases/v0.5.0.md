# Game Card Creator v0.5.0

**Release date:** 2026-11-16

Equal title bars for skill and spell cards, and a band that never ends above its icon. With the new
switches off, cards are as in 0.4.0, except a band whose icon reaches below it (see Changed behaviour).
Desktop only.

## Features

- **Equal title bars** (US-011, backlog #35): a switch per kind, `Skill titles` and `Spell titles` on the
  page `Page and text size`, each with `Title size` (default 9.1 pt, 6 to 20) and `Smallest title size`
  (default 7 pt, 4 to the title size), shown while the switch is on. With the switch on, every card of that
  kind in the run gets **one title size**: the largest size, from the title size down to the smallest in
  steps of the text size step, at which every title of the kind fits on one line (beside its icons). Every
  card of the kind also gets **one header height**: the tallest header of the kind, so bands and the rules
  of plain headers line up across the sheet. The header no longer shrinks with a card's text; the text is
  still fitted under it.
- **A title too long even at the smallest size** wraps, all headers of its kind grow with it, and the run
  gives one warning per kind, `SKILL_TITLE_WRAPPED` or `SPELL_TITLE_WRAPPED`, naming the note with the widest
  title: `1 of 6 titles wrap at 7 pt`.
- Cards of the other kind, and creature cards, are not changed.

## Changed behaviour

- **The band of a card with a branch ends at least 1 mm under its lowest icon** (US-009 AC-3 amended, found
  in the manual check of this release): before, a band ended 2 mm under the header only, and an icon taller
  than the header + 2 mm stuck out under it (for example a spell whose text shrank to 6 pt). Cards whose icon
  ends well above the band's edge are unchanged; a short header beside a tall icon gets a slightly deeper
  band, and its text starts lower. With equal title bars on, the icons count towards the kind's header
  height.

## Fixes

- The icon sticking out under a short band (see Changed behaviour).

## Breaking changes and migration

None. A settings file of 0.4.0 reads as before; the new settings take their defaults (the switches off).

## Known limits

- **Tested on:** Obsidian 1.15.0 on macOS (Apple silicon), in a scratch vault (manual check M09 and its
  re-check: the switch on an open tab, a refused size, equal titles and bands for skills and spells, a title
  too long even at the smallest size). **Not tested:** Windows, Linux, mobile, other Obsidian versions, your
  own vault's notes. `minAppVersion` stays 1.6.6.
- Equal mode measures every title and header of a kind once per run; in Node, 200 cards took about 2.5 to 3 s
  with it on against about 1 s off.
- Creature cards: their name band is drawn by Obsidian2Typst's statblock; equal bars there are a follow-up.
- `main.js` is about 15.8 MB (the compiler and two fonts are inside it).
